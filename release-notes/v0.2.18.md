## What's new in 0.2.18

- **SpaceMouse support (Windows):** pan, orbit, roll and zoom with a 3Dconnexion SpaceMouse while the 3D view is focused. Settings: right-click → 3D settings → Adjust → SpaceMouse (enable, roll, reverse motion, pan/zoom and rotation speed). Wheel zoom pauses while the cap moves to avoid double zoom from the driver.
- **TrueType text:** silkscreen text in TrueType fonts is drawn as filled glyphs (it showed up as outlines before).
- **Teardrops:** copper teardrop zones are rendered.
- **2D ↔ 3D:** switching between 2D and 3D takes the shortest rotation path; several turns in the 3D view no longer accumulate and replay.
- Settings changed just before the viewer closes are saved.

Many thanks to **@jrymk** for these fixes and the SpaceMouse support (#2)!
